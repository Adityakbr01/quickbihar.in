## Problem
29 category URLs exist in `sitemap-taxonomy.xml` (e.g., `/category/ethnic-wear`, `/category/sarees`, `/category/jeans`, `/category/jewellery`, etc.) but NONE are in `STATIC_ROUTES` (routes.ts). The `lastmod` dates on these sitemap URLs are old (mostly 2026-09-09, some 2026-10-14/18), suggesting they may not be regenerated with fresh content. Category pages may be client-side only rendered (not prerendered), which would make them soft 404s or thin content for crawlers.

## Supporting Evidence
- **Source:** Live `sitemap-taxonomy.xml` — 29 URLs with `lastmod` 2026-09-09 to 2026-10-18
- **Source:** `user-web/src/seo/routes.ts` — STATIC_ROUTES has 18 entries, 0 category pages
- **Source:** `user-web/src/seo/prerender.tsx` — no category route handling visible in main entry (only product, mall, jewelery product, location)
- **Source:** `user-web/src/seo/SeoRouter.tsx` — CategorySeo component exists (line 186) using `useCategoryBySlug` hook
- **Retrieval timestamp:** 2026-10-19

## Proposed Change
1. Verify if category pages are prerendered by fetching a sample (e.g., `/category/sarees`) and checking for prerender shell + JSON-LD in initial HTML
2. If NOT prerendered: Add category routes to prerender entry (`prerender.tsx`) using static category catalog (`categories-static.json`)
3. Add category routes to `STATIC_ROUTES` in `routes.ts` for proper crawl discovery
4. Ensure sitemap generation updates `lastmod` from actual content timestamps

## SEO/User Rationale
Category pages are high-value landing pages for broad queries (e.g., "sarees Buxar", "jeans Bihar"). If client-side only, Google may not index them properly (contributing to historical "Crawled - currently not indexed: 10"). Prerendering ensures crawlable HTML with proper meta, schema, and internal links.

## Expected Benefit
- Improved indexation of 29 category pages
- Better rankings for category-level queries
- Fresh `lastmod` dates in sitemap signal content freshness
