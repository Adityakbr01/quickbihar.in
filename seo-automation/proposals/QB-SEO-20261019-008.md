## Problem
Sitemap `lastmod` dates are stale:
- `sitemap-taxonomy.xml`: 29 URLs, mostly `2026-09-09` (40 days old)
- `sitemap-products.xml`: Mix of `2026-10-14`, `2026-10-18`, `2026-09-19`
- `sitemap-static.xml`: All `2026-10-19` (current - good)
- `sitemap-locations.xml`: All `2026-10-19` (current - good)
- `sitemap-malls.xml`: `2026-09-17` (stale)

Stale `lastmod` reduces crawl frequency signals and may cause Google to deprioritize recrawling.

## Supporting Evidence
- **Source:** Live sitemap XML files fetched 2026-10-19
- **Source:** Historical 2026-10-16 report — sitemap generation likely runs at build time
- **Retrieval timestamp:** 2026-10-19

## Proposed Change
1. Locate sitemap generation code (likely in `server/` or build scripts)
2. Ensure `lastmod` is set from:
   - Product/category/mall `updatedAt` or `lastModified` timestamp from database
   - Build timestamp for static pages
   - Current date for location pages (if no content timestamp)
3. Verify sitemap regeneration runs on every deploy (CI/CD pipeline shows it does)

## SEO/User Rationale
Fresh `lastmod` tells Google when content actually changed, optimizing crawl budget and ensuring timely re-indexing of updated pages.

## Expected Benefit
- More efficient Google crawling
- Faster indexation of updated content
- Accurate freshness signals
